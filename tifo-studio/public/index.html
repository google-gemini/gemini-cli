Scenes
    Workflow
    1. Script → Scenes: one paragraph per scene, AI voice optional.
    2. Insert XIs, then drag players at different playhead times to animate.
    3. Add arrows, zones, shadows, spotlights, labels timed to the words.
    4. Set camera zoom, 3D tilt and rotation per moment.
    5. Mix in cards, charts and illustrations.
    6. Export Video in up to 4K.
    Keys: Space play · K keyframe · ←/→ frame · Ctrl+C/V copy · Ctrl+D duplicate · Del delete
